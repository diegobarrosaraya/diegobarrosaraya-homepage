## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a watch movement's technical service sheet. One idea: precision you can inspect, every part specified, with tolerances and service records as the proof. It refuses the category default of a centred hero with a photo, a tagline and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool rhodium-white ground (#EDF0F2), plate (#E2E6E9), ink black hairlines and double-rule title blocks. Heat-blued steel (#1F3F8F) only on things you can click, ruby (#A3142D) only on the active part. Dark mode is a lume view: black ground (#060807), only active parts and primary actions glow green (#A6F58A). Barlow Condensed for labels and headings, Barlow for body, JetBrains Mono only for refs, part numbers and data. Tables with hairline rows, numbered callouts, a line drawing with no fills beyond the plate tone.

STORY: The visitor understands that Diego builds systems that are safe to run inside the constraints they come with, believes it because each service record names the constraint, safeguard and result, and then opens a record or the resume PDF.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title block across the top (name, "Cal. DBA", numbered nav, Lume view toggle) over a double rule. Below, left column about 55% wide: an exploded line drawing of a movement with five numbered callouts, one per work area. Right column about 45%: the headline at 30 to 44px condensed, a lede, a five-row spec table (Function, Tolerance, Regulation, Adaptation, Base), then two actions: "Read the service records" as the primary steel button and "Resume PDF" as the outline button.

FORM: Technical service sheet with exploded movement drawing, chosen by Diego on 2026-11-02, seed key af12aab7.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
